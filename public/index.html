For the selling dealer

Shipping a firearm to us on behalf of a customer? Here's what we need and how to reach us. We answer licensee requests the same business day.

  1. Contact us before you ship. A package arriving with no paperwork and no heads-up is the single most common cause of a delayed transfer.
  2. Request our signed FFL copy by phone at (717) 953-6359. We send license copies to licensed dealers only, not to non-licensees.
  3. Put the customer's name and your order number on the invoice inside the package, so we can match it to the right transfer request.
  4. Ship to the licensed premises at the address on our FFL. We'll confirm receipt and contact the customer the day it lands.

Good to know

Common questions

What do I bring to pick up my firearm?

A valid, current photo ID with your correct address — a PA driver's license is easiest. If your license address is out of date, bring a supporting document. You'll fill out the ATF Form 4473 here when you collect it.

How long does the background check take?

Most checks clear while you wait, usually in a few minutes. Occasionally the state comes back with a delay that's out of our hands — if that happens we'll explain exactly where things stand before you leave.

What does a transfer cost?

Give us a call at (717) 953-6359 for current transfer rates. It's a flat fee per firearm, paid at pickup.

Does the seller need anything from you?

Yes — a copy of our FFL. Submit the transfer request above and we'll send it to them directly, so you're not stuck playing middleman.

I'm buying or selling privately with someone I know — can you help?

Yes, and in Pennsylvania you generally have to. State law requires private handgun transfers to go through a licensed dealer or your county sheriff. Both people come in together, we run the paperwork and the background check, and you're done in one visit. Call (717) 953-6359 and we'll walk you through it — it's more routine than it sounds.

Can I buy a firearm on this website?

No. Nothing is sold or paid for online. This site is for starting transfers and sending us inquiries — every sale, background check, and payment happens in person at the shop.

Do you handle suppressors and SBRs?

We do — suppressors and short-barrelled rifles both. As of 1 January 2026 the federal tax stamp on suppressors and SBRs is $0, down from $200, and electronic Form 4 approvals are now typically running days rather than months. Everything else is unchanged: you still file a Form 4, still pass a background check, still submit fingerprints and a photo, and you still can't take it home until it's approved. Call (717) 953-6359 and we'll walk you through it.
